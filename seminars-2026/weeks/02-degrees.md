# 02 — Same degrees, different networks

**90 minutes; first PHOEG pilot.** Prerequisites: degree, connectedness, isomorphism definition. Paper first; PHOEG in the tool-aware block. The announced proof-clinic response is individual and protected.

You will realize a degree sequence, distinguish one construction from complete enumeration, and use an invariant to prove non-isomorphism.

## A — Foundation

Use handshaking to reject (3,3,3,2). Explain why an even degree sum alone is insufficient for graphicality. Perform one Havel–Hakimi reduction on (3,2,2,2,1).

## B — Core studio: two infrastructure reports

**Brief:** both teams report the degrees (2,2,2,1,1). Are their networks necessarily isomorphic? Necessarily equally connected?

1. Build a realization with five vertex cards. Trade drawings and search for a genuinely different structure, not a relabeling.
2. Predict the number of isomorphism classes before opening PHOEG.
3. In [PHOEG](https://phoeg.umons.ac.be/phoeg/), set x = **Size**, y = **Maximum degree**, choose order 5, constrain **Minimum degree** to exactly 1, and inspect (4,2). Explain why these conditions specify the intended degree multiset.
4. Check the degrees and component counts of the displayed graphs by hand. Record an evidence card.
5. Prove that your list is complete. Hint sequence: what are the possible components when every degree is 1 or 2? How many components can have endpoints? How many vertices remain for cycles?
6. Add **Connectedness = true**. Predict what survives before querying. Relate your observation to what Havel–Hakimi does and does not promise.

## C — Challenge

Classify all simple graph realizations of (2,2,2,2,2,2) up to isomorphism without brute force. Or use the Havel–Hakimi exchange argument to explain why the first greedy reduction preserves graphicality.

## Session rhythm

7 recall; 10 degree cards; 20 construction/query; 15 compare; 18 completeness proof; 13 connectedness change and individual clinic; 7 exit ticket. The clinic uses the same skills after exploration, not a take-home enumeration program.

**Evidence:** two constructions or a justified uniqueness claim, a completeness argument, and a statement of the search scope. If the site is unavailable, the teacher supplies drawings after your initial construction attempt.
