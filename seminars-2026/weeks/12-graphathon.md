# 12 — Graphathon: explain, certify, adapt

**90 minutes.** Teams of 3–4. Six team points and six individual points using the common rubric. Exact instance cards and individual perturbations arrive in the session.

## A — Foundation

Review model, method, invariant, certificate, runtime, and interpretation. Explain the difference between an optimum, a feasible result, a heuristic estimate, and an unresolved conjecture. No preparatory project is submitted.

## B — Choose one studio track

| Track | Brief | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Campus connections | Connect all locations cheaply under installation constraints | Feasible tree/network and an optimality argument or clearly bounded gap |
| Transit disruption | Route after a closure or changed edge rule | Algorithm choice, route and distance/correctness evidence |
| Placement office | Allocate scarce eligible places | Matching with cover or Hall-type obstruction where appropriate |
| Sensor coverage | Cover all locations within a budget | Model, feasible set, lower bound or exact-search evidence |
| Conjecture clinic | Investigate a graph-property claim using PHOEG/House of Graphs | Search scope, checked witness, and proof/refutation or precise unresolved status |

The teacher supplies comparable small instances. Team members rotate operator, skeptic, modeler, and explainer; nobody owns only the slides. Paper is sufficient. No original research result is required.

Submit a one-page board/card with (1) the model and limitations, (2) the method and key justification, (3) result and certificate/test, and (4) one changed assumption you explored. A visualization is optional; it must clarify the mathematics.

## C — Optional depth

After completing the core, strengthen a bound, explain a reduction, prove an equality case, or compare two modeling choices. This does not increase the published maximum score or replace missing core evidence.

## Schedule and individual evidence

0–10 min briefing and roles; 10–45 solve; 45–60 finalize evidence; 60–70 paired poster exchanges; 70–85 protected individual transfer; 85–90 collection/reflection.

Teams explain to another team in parallel, so the schedule does not require every group to give a serial presentation. The teacher collects the team card and each individual response. Oral sampling is possible only within staffing capacity. The teacher-held transfer changes an assumption or input and asks for a justified adaptation, not a recollection of the team's slide.
