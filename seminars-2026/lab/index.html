Graph

One edge per line: u v or u v weight. A single name adds an isolated vertex. Start with a line directed for arcs. Use ; as separator for names with spaces (Karlsplatz ; Stephansplatz). A line --- starts a second graph, for comparing two graphs side by side.

Export encodes the graph currently on the board (edges only, weights and directions are dropped). Paste it into House of Graphs “search by graph6” to look the graph up.
