# Optional challenge menu

These are alternatives for advanced pairs, not another required syllabus. Use an evidence card and conclude with a proof, checked counterexample, or precise unresolved statement. Teacher feedback rewards clarity, not novelty or speed.

## Depot placement: radius versus diameter

PHOEG: x = **Radius**, y = **Diameter**, **Connectedness = true**, orders 5–7. Predict a relation before inspecting the boundary. Prove r ≤ d ≤ 2r using a center and the triangle inequality. Find equality examples. Does a center necessarily minimize total distance? Consider a path a-b-c-d-e with four extra leaves at a and compute both objectives.

## The exceptions to a coloring rule

PHOEG: **Maximum degree** versus **Chromatic number**, connected graphs, orders 5–8. Investigate χ ≤ Δ. Find exceptional families; prove χ ≤ Δ+1 by greedy coloring. Formulate Brooks' theorem with its exclusions, and ask for the advanced proof scaffold rather than assuming the scatterplot proves it. This adapts the [PHOEG developers' teaching example](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.27242v1).

## An extremal theorem from data

PHOEG: **Size** versus **Chromatic number**, clique number at most 2, orders 4–8. Conjecture the maximum edge count. Prove Mantel's bound with the hints d(u)+d(v) ≤ n for each edge uv, double counting, and Cauchy–Schwarz. Construct a graph meeting the bound. Separately justify any claim about all equality cases.

## Symmetry breaking

Choose a small graph and determine its automorphisms. Find a pointwise fixing set. Compare brute-force permutations with structural pruning. Connect to asymmetric-depth research only after defining exactly which notion is being measured; do not identify ordinary automorphism-group size with a different invariant.

## Optimization certificates

Prove that endpoints of a maximal matching form a vertex cover, and use a maximum-matching lower bound to obtain a factor-two guarantee. Or compare an exact domination solution with a relaxation bound on a small graph. State the objective and distinguish upper from lower bounds.
