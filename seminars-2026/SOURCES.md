# Teaching sources, provenance, and reuse

The studio prompts, synthetic inputs, and scaffolding in this folder were newly prepared for the redesign of [Ján Pastorek's graph seminar](https://github.com/JanPastorek/1-AIN-413-22-Graphs). They adapt the existing course topics; they do not copy another institution's assessed questions.

| Source | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| [Oxford Graph Theory 2025–26](https://courses.maths.ox.ac.uk/course/view.php?id=6064) | Foundation/Core/Challenge separation |
| [Cambridge Mathematics schedules](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/files/schedules.pdf) | Manageable sheets and discussion in pairs; no claim to reproduce Cambridge staffing |
| [MIT 6.006 recitation resources](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/pages/resource-index/) | Preparation separated from coached problem solving |
| [MIT 18.304 assignments, 2015](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-304-undergraduate-seminar-in-discrete-mathematics-spring-2015/pages/assignments/) | Rehearsed mathematical explanation, peer questions, revision |
| [ETH Algorithms Lab 2025](https://cadmo.ethz.ch/education/lectures/HS25/algolab/) | Story → model → algorithm → implementation |
| [Princeton COS 226 assignments, Spring 2026](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spring26/cos226/assignments.php) | Live code understanding, with tests used for feedback |
| [PHOEG](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.27242v1) | Invariant exploration and theorem discovery; Brooks activity is adapted from the developers' teaching example |
| [House of Graphs](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23070v1) | Interesting witnesses, graph identity, and explicit distinction from exhaustive collections |

University practice descriptions were checked in August 2026. The MIT course pages are archives; none is presented as a current institution-wide AI policy. The combination here is a local design, not an endorsement by those universities.

All input graphs in `data/studio_graphs.json` are newly constructed mathematical examples. They are **synthetic**, not measured transport, evacuation, or wireless data. The historical Vienna dataset and other original datasets have not been copied or relicensed. An authentic-data extension should first confirm provenance, licensing, units, missing values, and suitability for teaching.

No blanket license is assigned by this change. The repository owner should choose a license for newly authored teaching/code material and separately establish the rights for third-party material before broader redistribution. Links provide attribution; they do not themselves grant permission to copy worksheets or datasets.
