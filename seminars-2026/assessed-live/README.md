# Live practicals: what to expect

Two eight-point practicals replace ordinary studio work; they are not take-home assignments. Exact instances and protected questions are released by the teacher at the session. This folder intentionally contains no future assessed data or solutions.

## Practical 1 — routing and evidence

Given a small network and operational brief, choose an algorithm, obtain a route, and justify why it is optimal. Then respond individually to a changed weight, direction, or query. BFS/Dijkstra/Bellman–Ford assumptions, a distance certificate, and runtime reasoning are relevant. A starter implementation may be provided so boilerplate does not dominate.

## Practical 2 — coloring code review

Given a small conflict network, complete or inspect a backtracking coloring method, test it, and explain a search/pruning choice. During the protected individual block, interpret a new constraint and distinguish finding a coloring from proving that fewer colors are impossible. This replaces the old map-coloring homework as evidence of understanding.

## Practical 1 schedule (Week 5)

10 min readiness and task reading; 30 min paired tool-aware work; 15 min checking/repair; 25 min protected individual response; 10 min collection and debrief. The short exit ticket is included in the protected response, with separate questions and marks.

## Practical 2 schedule (Week 9)

7 min readiness; 15 min planarity clinic (5 paired exploration, 10 protected writing); 25 min paired coloring-code review using supplied working starter code; 25 min protected individual practical; 8 min exit ticket; 10 min debrief. Planarity/induction and the backtracking principle must have been covered in lecture or preparation. This replaces the full ordinary Week 9 studio; it does not fit on top of it.

Short teacher reviews occur during the pair blocks. Every student submits the same categories of individual evidence, even if not orally sampled. If prerequisite teaching has not happened, postpone the assessed check and explicitly reallocate time; do not turn new material into an unannounced test.

Tools are specified on the day. Assessment follows the [eight-point rubric](../rubrics/README.md). Accessibility adjustments and an offline option are arranged in advance. Do not assume that practice graphs or public smoke tests are the assessment instances.
